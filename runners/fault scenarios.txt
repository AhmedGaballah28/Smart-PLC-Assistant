4 Realistic Fault Injection Test Scenarios
These simulate real industrial failures that cascade over time. Run each command with ~60 seconds between them to let the aggregator capture the progression.

Scenario 1: Cooling System Failure — Thermal Cascade (Line 1)
Story: Line 1 chiller loses refrigerant. Spindles heat up first, then heat spreads through the enclosed cabinets to downstream stations. Line 2 stays healthy — agent should notice the asymmetry.

1Af1 1 → 1Bf1 1 → 1Af1 3 → 1Bf1 3 → 11f1 2 → 12f1 3 → 12f6 2 → 16f1 3 → 16f6 2 → 1Af1 5 → 17f1 3
Step	Command	What Happens
1	1Af1 1	MC-A spindle bearing preload shift — slight warmth, 15% slower
2	1Bf1 1	MC-B follows — both machining centers warming
3	1Af1 3	MC-A escalates to 55°C — thermal compensation active, 60% slower
4	1Bf1 3	MC-B same — both machining centers now visibly degraded
5	11f1 2	Station 1 belt motor cabinet at 45°C — fan 100%, inspection 25% slower
6	12f1 3	Station 2 stepper missing steps — assembly significantly slower
7	12f6 2	Gripper vacuum cup softening from heat — vacuum level dropping
8	16f1 3	QC vision CPU throttling at 85°C — frame rate drops to 15fps
9	16f6 2	Vision focus drifting from thermal expansion — wrong classifications
10	1Af1 5	MC-A CRITICAL: approaching bearing seizure — 2.5× cycle time
11	17f1 3	Sorting actuator seal expanding — arm sluggish
What the agent should detect:

Temperature rising across multiple Line 1 stations
Production rate dropping on Line 1 only
Line 2 healthy = confirms it's not a global issue
QC pass_rate dropping from vision errors
Root cause: upstream machining centers were first to degrade
